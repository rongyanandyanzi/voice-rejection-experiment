**While the manager reads your suggestion**

Some further facts about the staffing situation at Aetheria Gardens:

- **Visitor numbers.** On a typical off-season weekday the park receives around 500 visitors. On a
  peak-season day it receives around 5,000. The busiest hour at the entrance is 10:00 to 11:00.
- **Entrance operations.** At peak, the queue for ticket checking at the main entrance can reach 30 to
  45 minutes at the busiest hour. In the off-season, several entrance staff have long stretches with no
  visitors to serve.
- **The workforce.** Almost all staff are full-time permanent employees on year-round contracts. Labour
  is the park's largest cost. The current labour plan was agreed by the management team for the whole
  year.
- **Records the park keeps.** A weekly visitor forecast (from bookings and previous years), a daily
  entrance queue log, and daily staffing rosters and payroll.
- **Training.** New entrance staff need a short induction of about two days before they work on their
  own.
